# ODD Feature: F18 Module 11 — Analytics

## Objective
Give the pharmacy business analytics on top of its sales, cost and stock data: ABC rotation matrix, rotation and days of inventory, stockouts and shortage alerts, margin and profitability by product, laboratory and branch, and an executive dashboard with a simple demand forecast.

## Problem / why
Module 11 is 1/6 in `ESTADO_FUNCIONALIDADES.md` (only the tenant stock report exists). Plan feature keys already exist in `0015_core_saas.sql`: `reports.basic` (all plans), `analytics.profitability` (Profesional, Premium), `analytics.abc` ("ABC y predictiva", Premium); COMPLETO has all. `sale_items` does not snapshot cost, so historical margin cannot be computed today.

## Scope
API:
- Migration `0033_analytics.sql`: `sale_items.unit_cost_base_bob` (nullable, snapshot of the presentation's average cost per base unit at confirm); permission `analytics.read` (owner, regente, encargado) in migration, `role-templates.ts` and `seed.ts`; `stock_alerts` table for shortage alerts (tenant, branch, presentation, kind `OUT_OF_STOCK | LOW_COVERAGE`, days of stock, opened/resolved/acknowledged) with idempotent open-alert unique index.
- Shared helper for net sales velocity (net of returns, excluding voided) and available stock per branch/presentation; reorder service keeps its behavior.
- Endpoints under `api/v1/analytics` (`analytics.read`; branch filter or all branches the user can access, like the tenant stock report):
  - `abc` (`analytics.abc`): classification by net revenue (A 80 % / B 15 % / C 5 % cumulative) plus units; period from/to.
  - `rotation` (`analytics.profitability`): per presentation avg daily units, available, days of inventory, turnover (cost of goods sold ÷ current stock value), no-movement items.
  - `stockouts` (`analytics.profitability`): current out-of-stock and low-coverage items; alerts list + acknowledge; scheduler reusing the inventory alert pattern.
  - `profitability` (`analytics.profitability`): revenue net of returns, cost, margin and margin % grouped by product, laboratory or branch; lines without cost snapshot fall back to current average cost and are flagged as estimated.
  - `dashboard` (`reports.basic` for KPIs; forecast section only with `analytics.abc`): sales, tickets, average ticket, margin (if plan), out-of-stock count, near-expiry value, top products; forecast of next 30 days demand per top presentation and projected stockout date.
Web: sidebar section "Analítica" with pages for each report, CSV export where tabular, plan-locked links like staff/customers navs.

## Provisional defaults (REGISTRO_DECISIONES D72-D76, pending Harold)
- D72: margin uses the cost snapshotted on each sale line (average cost at confirm). Sales before this change use the current average cost and are marked "estimated". Depends on D08/D40.
- D73: ABC by net revenue in the period with cutoffs 80/15/5 % cumulative; units shown for reference.
- D74: days of inventory = available ÷ average daily units of the last 30 days (net of returns); low coverage when < 7 days; out of stock when available = 0 and it sold in the last 30 days.
- D75: "predictive" = demand forecast with a moving average plus linear trend over the last 8 weeks, with projected stockout date; no machine learning.
- D76: permission `analytics.read` (owner, regente, encargado); reports cover all branches the user has access to, with branch filter.

- Scheduler: `StockAlertScheduler` (STOCK_ALERT_INTERVAL_MINUTES, default 60; first run after 30 s; off when `NODE_ENV==='test'` or interval <= 0). `StockAlertsService.scan()` returns `{ tenants, created, resolved }`: picks pharmacies with `analytics.profitability` (plan, COMPLETO flag or override; ACTIVE, TRIALING in trial or PAST_DUE in grace), per active branch computes shortages with the same helpers as the report, upserts open alerts (refreshing days/available, `on conflict ... where resolved_at is null`) and resolves open ones that no longer apply. One transaction per pharmacy; a failing pharmacy is logged and skipped.
- Decisions: out of stock = net base sold in the last 30 La Paz days > 0 and available = 0 (fully returned sales do not count); low coverage = available > 0 and available / (net sold / 30) < 7; `suggestedBase = ceil(avgDaily x 30 - available)` >= 0. Dashboard tickets = CONFIRMED + PARTIALLY_RETURNED sales (voided and fully RETURNED excluded); average ticket = net sales / tickets; `units` sums net units in each presentation's own unit; `outOfStockCount` counts (branch, presentation) pairs; near expiry = AVAILABLE batches in active warehouses expiring today..+30 days (already expired excluded), physical quantity at the current average cost, units without cost reported in `uncostedUnits`. Margin reuses `costing`/`toRow` (snapshot, else current average cost) and equals profitability totals. Forecast (D75): weeks are La Paz Monday-start, the current incomplete week is excluded; level = mean of the last 4 weeks, trend = least-squares slope over the 8 weeks, forecast for week k = max(0, MA4 + slope x (k + 1.5)); projected demand prorates the last partial week; stockout day = first day cumulative forecast demand >= available (day 0 when already out with demand; null with no demand or beyond 365 days); negative net weeks are clamped to 0. Forecast and stockout days are in BASE units (use `baseUnitFactor`).
